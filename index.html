PROLOGUE · BEFORE YOU READ

One housewarming. Three marriages. One social circle.

Episode 01 begins at Sana and Mehul’s housewarming. Everyone here is an adult, and the story follows how familiar marriages, friendships, family ties and outside attention begin to overlap during one evening.

Central couple

Mira Sharma, 22 × Aarav Malhotra, 23

Married. Their relationship is soft, familiar and physically easy; Aarav is Mira’s normal baseline for everyday intimacy.

Central couple

Rhea Kapoor, 23 × Kabir Sethi, 24

Married. Their rhythm is sharper—teasing, competitive and openly jealous when something catches their attention.

Central couple

Naina Mehra, 22 × Dev Arora, 23

Married. They are direct and explicit with each other, especially around touch, questions and boundaries.

Family & married friends

Sana Qureshi, 27 is married to Mehul Suri, 29; they are hosting the housewarming.

Neha Kapoor, 29 is Rhea’s elder sister and is married to Arjun Rao, 31.

Rohan Sethi, 32 is Kabir’s elder brother and is married to Tara Sethi, 28, making Tara Kabir’s bhabhi.

Teachers / future-use tracks

Ananya Rao, 25 is an adult independent tutor/coach with an established future track connected to Aarav. Their dynamic is built around familiarity, trust, playful control and slow-burn tension—not grades or academic leverage.

Meera Khanna, 24 is an adult independent tutor/coach with a future track connected to Kabir. The relationship remains independent and consensual, never tied to marks, attendance or reward/punishment.

Shalini Bose is an adult independent tutor/coach with a future track connected to Dev. Her exact age has not yet been fixed in canon, so the UI intentionally does not invent one.

Other adults you’ll meet

Ishaan Verma, 19 is a photography student helping with the photo setup.

Yash Mehta, 19 is an architecture student helping with the apartment setup.

Armaan Das, 19 is a design student helping with cables and equipment.

Sameer Bedi, 40 is Rhea’s former college debate instructor, now with no authority over her.

Vikram Khanna, 58 and Prakash Menon, 57 are older residents/acquaintances in the building social circle.

Reader orientation: the episode stays focused on Mira, Rhea and Naina. The other characters matter because of who knows whom, who is married to whom, and who notices what during the same evening.
